**Platform:** IG feed and Threads. Works as a Story if letterboxed.

## Why this
A "what we need to be watching this month" list went around and named ten
Black shows. Nine were already in the catalog; the tenth (The Black Hamptons)
was added the same day. Every one of them is votable right now and almost none
of them carry a verdict yet, so the flyer's whole argument is true: the list
exists everywhere, the ruling exists nowhere.

Every poster is real TMDB key art for a title actually in the catalog, and
every status line was re-checked against it. No scores appear anywhere, because
almost none of these have one, and inventing one would be the exact thing the
site refuses to do.

## Caption

Everybody and they mama posted the September list. Ten shows, all ours, all
worth the time. Nobody said whether any of it was actually good. 👀

That part is on us. The Drop, Married Again, A Different World, Beauty in
Black, Diarra, Average Joe, Forever, Black Hamptons, Divorced Sistas, Varnell
Hill. Ten shows and not one verdict between them.

Pick the one you already finished and rule on it. Seasoned or sent back, one
tap, no account. Then come tell us which one we got wrong.

itswellseasoned.com 🍿

## Participation mechanic
Ten targets, one tap each. The comment ask ("which one did we get wrong") is
deliberately an argument rather than a poll, because a disagreement gets a
reply and a poll gets a scroll. Lands on /#/dropped, where all ten sit in one
board with the buttons already on screen.
